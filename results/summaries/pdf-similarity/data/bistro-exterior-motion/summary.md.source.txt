# PDF controlled comparison

Each value is a mean across independent seeds 1/2/3; SD is the sample standard deviation of the three seed means. Frames/pixels are averaged within each phase and seed first. Paired differences are PDF on minus PDF off. Where reference sensitivity is assessed, a resolved direction requires its paired difference to exceed the change measured with independent half-stream and previous-checkpoint references. Fixed-reference policy rows explicitly mark sensitivity NOT EVALUATED.

Static image errors use frame 128; static GPU cost averages frames 41–128. Dynamic errors use representative frames 32, 48 and 96 separately. TRMAE is local to pairs 31→32, 47→48 and 95→96, respectively; it is not a video-average metric. GPU costs independently average every timing in the corresponding full phases 1–32, 33–64 and 65–96. Total gpu_ms is the recorded total including all instrumented passes; initial/spatial costs are separate diagnostics. Logical 30 FPS is the playback interval, not a performance claim.
