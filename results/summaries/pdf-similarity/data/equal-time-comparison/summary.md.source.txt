# Bounded equal-time comparison

Targets are midpoints of the measured GPU-time range shared by all six seed/method curves in each view. Values at each target are piecewise-linear interpolated estimates, rather than measured exact-time configurations. GPU time averages frames 41–128; errors use frame 128. Each paired difference is PDF on minus off; mean and sample SD use independent seeds 1/2/3. SMAPE is percent and its paired difference is percentage points.

Points are individual seeds; error bars are sample SD across three seeds, and labels give neighbor budgets. The CSV tables retain every measured point, each interpolation bracket/weight, and its closest achieved configuration/time. Reference directions, where assessed, use matching per-seed current, half-stream and previous-checkpoint values interpolated at the same target; a direction is resolved only when the paired mean exceeds its largest reference-induced change.
