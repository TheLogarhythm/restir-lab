__SEQUENCE__

Evaluation scope

8 main conditions and 3 independent method seeds. The 78 runs comprise 48 main experiment runs, 6 spatial-only ablation runs and 24 additional neighbor-budget runs. Resolution is 1920×1080; the other main experiment settings are held constant.

The 15 reference states use two policies. The 5 Cornell/Arcade states passed the original two-stream precision acceptance checks. The 10 Bistro states use seed 1001 and a fixed 262,144 local samples. The original precision, uncertainty and sensitivity checks remain unevaluated for Bistro. Bistro results are observations relative to these fixed-sample references.

The PDF experiment images are undenoised display PNGs from seed 1 at fixed exposure; error metrics use linear HDR. Tables use method seeds 1/2/3. The ± values are sample standard deviations, not confidence intervals. Paired differences are PDF on minus PDF off; negative error differences mean lower error. 30 FPS is the logical and playback rate; GPU time is measured separately.

Neighbor budgets and equal-time interpolation

Both static Bistro views are tested with 2/4/8 neighbors. Piecewise linear interpolation uses the midpoint of the GPU-time interval shared by all seed/method curves, with no extrapolation.

For the interior view, the measured 2-neighbor PDF off mean outperforms every measured PDF on point in all three error metrics and GPU cost. A favorable interpolated value at the common time therefore does not establish an overall cost advantage. Bistro reference precision remains unevaluated.
